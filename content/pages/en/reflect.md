  - label: A question, if you wish
    paragraphs:
      - What is quietly present in you just now — not the story you might tell, but the feeling itself? Can you let it be here, even for a breath or two?
  - label: If it feels right
    paragraphs:
      - Is there a sincere wish in you to be met by love — not to become someone else, but to be received as you are? You do not have to have the answer.
      - You might, if it feels gentle, let that wish open toward Divine Love. Not to become someone else, and not to force a feeling — simply to be received, as you are.
  - label: A gentle check-in
    paragraphs:
      - As you read, can you notice your body again — the weight of it, the breath moving? If something feels tender, can you meet it honestly, without needing it to change straight away?

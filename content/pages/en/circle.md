heading: What is a Circle of Light
intro:
  - "A Circle of Light is a quiet, welcoming space. We gather, in person or online, with one simple intention: to rest and to receive the Light."
  - The Light may move through every layer of who we are — body, feelings, mind and spirit. Each person receives what they are ready for. There is no performance, and you do not need to explain why you are here.
  - If it feels right, you might think of this as opening to Divine Love — not as something to reach for, but as a presence already here, meeting you as you are.
flow_title: How a circle flows
flow_steps:
  - We open with a short welcome and arrive together.
  - You may rest, close your eyes, and let the day soften, as you might let warmth settle on your face.
  - Tiago holds the space in silence, like a quiet prayer.
  - We close gently. If you would like to share, there is space for that. If not, that is perfectly fine too.
flow_duration: A circle lasts about an hour to an hour and a half. You may leave at any time. Please do not take part while driving.
flow_camera: Turning your camera on or off is your choice. Nothing is recorded.
notice_title: What you may notice
notice_text: Everyone’s experience is different, and there is no right way to receive. Some people feel warmth, rest or a quieter mind; some fall asleep; some notice very little in the moment. Whatever arises is welcome.
notice_care: This is a complementary spiritual practice. It does not replace medical or psychological care, and it does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. Please keep seeing your doctor or therapist whenever you need to.
before_after_title: Before and after
before_title: Before
before_text: You do not need to prepare anything. If you like, find a calm place where you will not be disturbed, and sit or lie down. A glass of water and a blanket nearby can help.
after_title: After
after_text: Afterwards, give yourself a few quiet minutes. Drink some water, and be gentle with yourself. If feelings or questions come up, you are welcome to write.

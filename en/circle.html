What is a Circle of Light

A Circle of Light is a quiet, welcoming space. We gather, in person or online, with one simple intention: to rest and to receive the Light.

The Light may move through every layer of who we are — body, feelings, mind and spirit. Each person receives what they are ready for. There is no performance, and you do not need to explain why you are here.

If it feels right, you might think of this as opening to Divine Love — not as something to reach for, but as a presence already here, meeting you as you are.

Tiago holds the space, like a quiet prayer. You may sit or lie down, close your eyes, and let yourself be. Turning your camera on or off is your choice. Nothing is recorded.

What you may notice

Everyone's experience is different, and there is no right way to receive. Some people feel warmth or deep rest. Some notice a quieter mind, a softer heart, or a sense of lightness. Some fall asleep. Some notice very little in the moment. Whatever arises is welcome.

This is a complementary spiritual practice. It does not replace medical or psychological care, and it does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. Please keep seeing your doctor or therapist whenever you need to.

Before and after your circle

Before

Find a calm, comfortable place where you won't be disturbed, indoors or outdoors, wherever feels right. You may lie down or sit. Switch your phone to flight mode, and keep a glass of water and a blanket nearby. Have a pen and notebook with you too. If you feel like writing, it can help you remember what came through.

During

You may sense, feel, hear, see or simply know things. Insights may come, or just deep rest. Whatever comes through is welcome, and there's no right way to receive.

After

Give yourself a few quiet minutes before you return to your day. Drink some water, and be gentle with yourself for the rest of the day. If feelings or questions come up, I'm here. You can reach me by message, call or video call, so you keep feeling held and heard.

A question, if you wish

What is quietly present in you just now — not the story you might tell, but the feeling itself? Can you let it be here, even for a breath or two?

If you would like a little more space with the question, there is a page of pauses and reflections, to use in your own time. Visit Pause and Reflect, if you like.

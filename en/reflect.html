Pause and Reflect

These questions are only invitations. There is nothing to get right, and no need to answer out loud. If you like, you might take them slowly, one at a time, and leave a little space between them.

A quiet reflection

If you like, can you let this moment be enough?

A small pause

If you like, take one slow breath. Let your shoulders drop. Notice your body, just as it is in this moment. You have arrived.

A question, if you wish

What is quietly present in you just now — not the story you might tell, but the feeling itself? Can you let it be here, even for a breath or two?

If it feels right

Is there a sincere wish in you to be met by love — not to become someone else, but to be received as you are? You do not have to have the answer.

You might, if it feels gentle, let that wish open toward Divine Love. Not to become someone else, and not to force a feeling — simply to be received, as you are.

A gentle check-in

As you read, can you notice your body again — the weight of it, the breath moving? If something feels tender, can you meet it honestly, without needing it to change straight away? Growth through love is often quiet, and it can begin with that simple honesty.

Honesty, met with kindness, can be a quiet path toward truth — not a truth you have to prove, but the truth of what is here, in this moment.

A few questions

If a question is already present in you, you might find it below. If not, you are welcome simply to read, and notice what stirs.

What is a Circle of Light?

A quiet online gathering where you rest and open to the healing energy. Tiago holds the Light; you do not need to do or fix anything.

Do I need to believe anything?

No. An open, respectful heart is enough. Curiosity, and a willingness to rest, are welcome.

What might I feel?

It varies. Some people feel relaxation, warmth or a quiet mind; some fall asleep; some notice little at the time. There is no right way to receive.

Is this medical treatment?

No. It is a complementary spiritual practice, and it does not replace medical or psychological care. Please continue any treatment you are receiving.

Is it online?

Yes. Circles of Light are offered online, and you are welcome from anywhere. Nothing is recorded, and your camera may be on or off.

Is there another question quietly waiting? You are welcome to write, if you wish.

Occasional reflections

A gentle reflection in your inbox every two weeks. No sales, unsubscribe anytime.

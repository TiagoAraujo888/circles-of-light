About Tiago

Long before healing found me, I was a volunteer firefighter, from my teenage years into my youth. Answering emergency calls taught me to stay present when someone is hurting, and to care without hesitation.

Later, my own struggles brought me to a place where I couldn't see the meaning of things, or how I could help make the world a little better. That search led me inward, and to energy healing. Today I hold space so that others can find their own way back to peace and to the Light within them.

My path is rooted in the love of Christ. I trust the Holy Spirit to guide each circle, and I simply hold the space. Whatever your own path or belief, you are welcome here, just as you are.

In a circle, there is nothing you need to do or explain. You simply rest, and the Light goes where your soul needs it most. Many people leave feeling calmer, lighter and more at peace, with a quiet sense of coming home to themselves.

This is a complementary spiritual practice. It does not replace medical or psychological care.
